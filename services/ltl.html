What It Is

Less-Than-Truckload with J&A Freight Systems

Less-than-truckload (LTL) shipping combines freight from several shippers onto one trailer. Your shipment travels through a carrier's terminal network, sharing linehaul costs with other freight. For shipments from one to roughly ten pallets, LTL is usually the most economical choice, and with the right partner it does not have to mean losing visibility.

The challenge with LTL is complexity: freight class, density, accessorials, reweighs, and carrier tariffs that change quarter to quarter. J&A manages that complexity for you. Our Transportation Management System rates your shipment across multiple LTL carriers at once, selects the best balance of cost and transit time, and produces a correct bill of lading so the carrier bills what we quoted.

Behind the software is the same accountable team that handles our truckload freight. A named J&A representative owns your LTL program, resolves exceptions, and reviews your lane data with you so recurring shipments keep getting cheaper and more reliable.
