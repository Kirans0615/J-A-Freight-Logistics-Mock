What It Is

Managed Transportation with J&A Freight Systems

Managed transportation is a program, not a transaction. Instead of quoting one load at a time, J&A takes responsibility for your transportation function: sourcing and managing carriers, planning daily shipments across modes, executing and tracking every load, auditing every invoice, and reporting results against targets you set together with us.

Mid-market shippers get enterprise-level freight management without hiring a transportation team or licensing their own software. Our best-in-class Transportation Management System becomes your system of record, your team keeps visibility and control through the portal, and a dedicated J&A group does the daily work.

Programs are built around your network: contract lanes bid to our vetted carrier base, spot freight covered through the same TMS, LTL and parcel rated and audited, and quarterly business reviews that turn shipping data into measurable savings on the next quarter's lanes.

How It Works

From quote to delivery in four steps

01
