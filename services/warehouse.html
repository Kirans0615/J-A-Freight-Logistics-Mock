What It Is

Warehouse with J&A Freight Systems

Warehousing from a 3PL is about more than square footage. It is receiving, put-away, inventory accuracy, order picking, and getting product back out the door on the right truck at the right time. J&A coordinates secure short-term and long-term storage through partner facilities, and because we also manage your freight, the warehouse and the trucks finally talk to each other.

Typical uses include overflow storage during seasonal peaks, staging inventory closer to customers, holding import containers until receiving docks are ready, cross-docking inbound truckloads into outbound LTL or parcel orders, and simple pick-and-pack fulfillment for B2B orders.

Your J&A representative manages the warehouse relationship, the inbound and outbound transportation, and the reporting, so you have one point of contact for the entire cycle instead of a separate vendor for each step.
