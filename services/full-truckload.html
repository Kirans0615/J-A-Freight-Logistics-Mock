What It Is

Full Truckload with J&A Freight Systems

Full truckload (FTL) means your freight has the trailer to itself. There are no shared-space stops, no cross-dock handling, and no transit-time surprises from someone else's pallets. For shipments of roughly ten pallets or more, high-value or fragile goods, or anything on a tight delivery appointment, FTL is usually the fastest and safest way to move it.

As a non-asset-based 3PL, J&A does not own trucks. That is the point. Instead of forcing your freight onto whatever equipment we happen to have parked, we source the right truck for each load from a network of roughly 200 carrier partners that we have vetted and continue to monitor. You get the coverage of a national fleet with the accountability of a single Chicago team that answers the phone.

Every FTL shipment is assigned a named J&A owner who books the carrier, confirms pickup, watches the load move in our Transportation Management System, and handles exceptions before they reach your dock.
