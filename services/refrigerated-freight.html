Refrigerated Freight
What It Is

Refrigerated Freight with J&A Freight Systems

Refrigerated freight, often called reefer freight, is any shipment that must stay within a defined temperature range from the moment it is loaded until it is signed for. Fresh produce, dairy, meat, frozen foods, beverages, pharmaceuticals, and certain chemicals all depend on it. A single excursion can mean a rejected delivery and a lost sale.

J&A's refrigerated division has moved temperature-sensitive freight since the 2000s. We match each load to carriers whose reefer units, trailer condition, and track record on temperature-controlled lanes have been vetted, and we confirm set points, pre-cooling, and continuous versus cycle operation before the truck arrives at your dock.

Throughout transit, your J&A owner watches the load in our Transportation Management System and stays in contact with the driver, so a delay at a receiver or a reefer alarm becomes a phone call to you rather than a surprise on the delivery receipt.

How It Works

Deliver and document
