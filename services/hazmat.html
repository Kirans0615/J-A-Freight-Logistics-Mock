What It Is

Hazmat with J&A Freight Systems

Hazardous materials (hazmat) shipping covers any commodity regulated under the U.S. Department of Transportation's Hazardous Materials Regulations, including flammable liquids, corrosives, compressed gases, oxidizers, certain batteries, and many industrial chemicals. Moving them requires hazmat-endorsed drivers, carriers with the proper registration and insurance, correct shipping papers and placards, and segregation rules that are easy to get wrong.

J&A has arranged hazmat freight for industrial and chemical shippers for decades. We maintain a vetted group of hazmat-qualified carriers, verify endorsements and insurance limits before tendering, and review your shipping description, packaging group, and emergency response information so the shipment is compliant before the driver is dispatched.

As with every J&A service line, a named representative owns the load from quote to delivery and tracks it in our Transportation Management System, so regulated freight moves with the same visibility as any other shipment.
