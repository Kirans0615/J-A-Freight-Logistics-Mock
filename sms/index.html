SMS Privacy Policy

How J&A Freight Systems collects, uses, and protects information related to our text messaging program.

Effective Date: June 25, 2026  ·  Last Updated: June 25, 2026

1. Program Description

J&A Freight Systems ("J&A," "we," "us," or "our") operates an SMS/text messaging program to communicate with shippers, carriers, and other business contacts regarding freight and logistics services. Messages sent through this program may include:

  • Shipment status updates and delivery notifications
  • Load confirmations, pickup and delivery alerts
  • Quote responses and rate confirmations
  • Carrier onboarding and compliance reminders
  • Appointment and scheduling confirmations
  • Account and portal access notifications
  • Transactional and operational service alerts

2. How You Opt In

You may enroll in our SMS program by:

  • Checking the SMS consent box on our contact or quote request form at jandafreight.com/contact/
  • Verbally or in writing providing consent to a J&A team member during the onboarding process
  • Texting a designated keyword to our SMS number as communicated during setup

By opting in, you confirm that you are the account holder or authorized user of the mobile number provided and that you consent to receive text messages from J&A Freight Systems at that number.

3. Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on your account activity and the nature of your engagement with J&A Freight Systems. You may receive multiple messages per shipment, load, or service request. Transactional messages are sent only as needed to support your logistics activity.

4. Message & Data Rates

Standard message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier plan. J&A Freight Systems does not charge any additional fee for SMS communications. Contact your wireless provider for details on your messaging plan.

5. How to Opt Out

You may opt out of receiving SMS messages from J&A Freight Systems at any time by replying STOP to any message you receive from us. After opting out, you will receive a one-time confirmation message and no further messages will be sent, except as required by law or to confirm your opt-out.

You may also opt out by contacting us directly at info@jandafreight.com or by calling (773) 205-7720 and requesting removal from our SMS list.

6. Help

To receive assistance with our SMS program at any time, reply HELP to any message you receive. You may also reach us directly:

  • Email: info@jandafreight.com
  • Phone: (773) 205-7720
  • Address: 4704 W. Irving Park Road, Suite B, Chicago, IL 60641

7. Information We Collect

When you opt in to our SMS program, we collect the following information:

  • Your mobile phone number
  • Your opt-in method, date, and time (consent record)
  • Message delivery and response data
  • Any information you provide in reply to our messages

This information is used solely to operate and improve our SMS communications and to maintain accurate opt-in and opt-out records as required by applicable law.

8. How We Use Your Information

Information collected through our SMS program is used exclusively to:

  • Deliver the text messages you have requested or consented to receive
  • Maintain compliance records of your consent
  • Respond to help or support requests submitted via SMS
  • Process opt-out requests promptly and accurately

We do not use SMS opt-in data for marketing profiling, advertising targeting, or any purpose unrelated to the direct delivery of the logistics communications you have consented to receive.

9. Data Sharing & Third Parties

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. The above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

We may share your mobile number and related SMS data only with:

  • SMS platform providers engaged solely to transmit messages on our behalf, under strict data processing agreements that prohibit any secondary use of your data
  • Legal or regulatory authorities when required by law, court order, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of J&A Freight Systems or others

Under no circumstances will your opt-in status, mobile number, or consent data be sold, rented, traded, or transferred to any third party for marketing, promotional, or advertising purposes.

10. Data Retention

We retain SMS opt-in and opt-out records for a minimum of four (4) years to comply with applicable telecommunications regulations and best practices. Message logs may be retained for operational and compliance purposes. Upon a verifiable opt-out request, your number will be suppressed from future messaging immediately.

11. Security

J&A Freight Systems implements reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect SMS-related data against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. Access to opt-in records is limited to personnel who require it to operate our messaging program.

12. Children's Privacy

Our SMS program is intended solely for business use by adults (18 years of age or older). We do not knowingly collect mobile information from individuals under 18. If we become aware that a minor has opted in without appropriate consent, we will promptly remove that number from our list.

13. Supported Carriers

Our SMS program is supported by major U.S. wireless carriers including, but not limited to, AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile, and Sprint. Carrier support may vary; message delivery is not guaranteed across all networks.

14. Changes to This Policy

J&A Freight Systems reserves the right to update or modify this SMS Privacy Policy at any time. Changes will be effective upon posting to this page. Continued participation in our SMS program after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

If you have questions about this SMS Privacy Policy or our text messaging practices, please contact us:
