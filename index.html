Who We Are

A Family-Built 3PL
with Enterprise Power

Founded in 1986 in Chicago, Illinois, J&A Freight Systems, Inc. is a non-asset-based third-party logistics provider built on the values of integrity, accountability, and relentless service. For nearly four decades, we have been the logistics backbone for mid-market manufacturers, distributors, and retailers shipping across North America.

Every member of our team undergoes ongoing logistics education workshops paired with seasoned transportation experts. We believe that knowledge-driven logistics is the difference between a broker and a true strategic partner — and our client relationships, many lasting over a decade, are proof.

Our strict carrier vetting policy ensures that only FMCSA-compliant, fully insured motor carriers enter the J&A network. Before any carrier touches your freight, they pass our rigorous multi-point safety, authority, and insurance verification process.

What We Do

Comprehensive Logistics Solutions

Full Truckload (FTL)

Dedicated full truckload capacity for time-sensitive shipments. Direct origin-to-destination service with no stops or transfers.

Less-Than-Truckload (LTL)

Cost-effective freight consolidation for smaller shipments. Share trailer space without sacrificing speed or reliability.

Intermodal Rail Services

Reduce costs on heavy long-haul corridors through rail partnerships. Seamless transitions between truck and rail modes.

Hazmat & Specialized Cargo

Certified hazardous materials handling and climate-controlled food-grade freight. Oversized flatbed and heavy-haul machinery coordination.

Warehousing & Distribution

Decentralized US warehouse and distribution center network. Facility management, overflow inventory, and yard logistics.

Supply Chain Visibility

24/7 live shipment tracking via MercuryGate TMS integration. Automated network modeling and real-time alerts.
