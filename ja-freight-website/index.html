Questions? We Have You Covered

Have questions? No worries. We have you covered.

We provide streamlined logistics solutions that move your business forward.

We offer Full Truckload (FTL), Less-Than-Truckload (LTL), refrigerated freight, drayage, intermodal, warehouse, and managed transportation — all backed by our vetted carrier network and MercuryGate TMS.

Yes. J&A Freight Systems is FMCSA registered and surety bonded to broker freight in all 48 contiguous states and select cross-border lanes. Our MC and DOT numbers are public record.

Every load is matched to a carrier that has passed our multi-point vetting — active authority, FMCSA safety score, and current insurance. We then track the shipment live through MercuryGate TMS with proactive check calls at key milestones.

Changes and cancellations are handled on a case-by-case basis depending on how close to the pickup window the request falls. Contact your dedicated J&A representative as early as possible for the best outcome.

All carriers in our network carry active cargo insurance. J&A also maintains its own broker bond. For high-value or specialized freight, we can arrange additional coverage — ask your rep at quote time.

Transit times depend on origin, destination, and mode. FTL typically runs 1–5 business days; LTL adds transit time based on the carrier's hub network. We provide estimated delivery windows at booking and keep you updated throughout.

Use the "Request a Quote" button on this page or call us at (773) 205-7720. Spot quotes turn around the same business day; contract lane pricing typically comes back within 24 hours.

Cross-border shipments typically require a commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading, and customs entry documents. Our team coordinates with licensed customs brokers and will walk you through the paperwork for your specific commodity and lane.

Once loaded, your shipment moves directly toward its destination under real-time GPS and ELD tracking. You'll receive automated milestone updates and can view live status any time through our TMS portal.

We invoice after delivery with standard net payment terms. Freight audit is included — we verify every invoice before it hits your AP. Most clients also receive quarterly business reviews showing spend, savings, and lane performance.
